The quality of a uPVC window depends on what is inside it. When Fabframe talks about high quality windows and doors, this is what it means:

Premium Lead-Free Profiles

Multi-chamber, UV-stabilised uPVC profiles resist yellowing, cracking and fading even under strong high-altitude sun.

Galvanised Steel Reinforcement

Steel inserts inside the profile add strength for large sashes and wind loads, and keep frames straight for years.

Welded joints and rubber gaskets create a continuous seal that blocks water, dust and draughts.

Branded Hardware

Smooth rollers, friction stays, handles and multi-point locking systems are tested for thousands of open-close cycles.

04Choosing the Right Glass for Your uPVC Windows

Glass makes up most of a window's surface, so it has a big effect on comfort and safety. Fabframe helps you pick the right glazing for each room:

Clear Float GlassBudget-friendly and clear. Good for interior or low-exposure openings.
Toughened Glass4–5x stronger than normal glass and breaks into blunt pieces. Ideal for doors and large panels.
Double Glazed UnitsTwo panes with an insulating gap, for the best thermal and sound control.
Reflective / Tinted GlassReduces glare and solar heat on sun-facing façades.
Frosted GlassLets light in but keeps privacy. Perfect for bathrooms and partitions.

08Simple Care Tips for Long-Lasting uPVC Windows

One of the biggest advantages of uPVC is how little care it needs. A few simple habits keep your windows like new:

Wipe frames monthlyUse a soft cloth with mild soap and water. Avoid harsh solvents and abrasive pads.
Clean the tracksVacuum dust from sliding tracks so the rollers move smoothly.
Lubricate hardwareA drop of silicone spray on hinges and locks once or twice a year is enough.
Check drainage slotsKeep the small drainage holes clear so rainwater can drain out freely.
