High Quality Windows and Doors Built for Himachal's Climate

Himachal's weather is tough on building materials. There is intense UV at altitude, heavy monsoon rain, freezing winter nights and big temperature swings between seasons. Fabframe makes high quality windows and doors that handle these conditions:

Multi-Chamber Profiles for Thermal Insulation

uPVC is a poor conductor of heat, and multi-chamber profiles trap air inside the frame. This cuts heat transfer, keeping rooms warmer in winter and cooler in summer and lowering the load on heaters and air conditioners.

Double Glazing for Noise and Energy Control

Double-glazed units (DGU) have two glass panes with a sealed air or gas gap between them. They noticeably reduce outside noise and heat loss, which suits bedrooms facing busy roads and homes in colder districts.

UV-Stabilised, Lead-Free Material

The profiles are UV-stabilised, so they don't turn yellow or go brittle in strong mountain sunlight. Because they are lead-free, they are safer for families and better for the environment.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do uPVC windows last?

Good-quality uPVC windows with steel reinforcement and branded hardware commonly last 25 years or more with very little maintenance. Occasional cleaning and a little lubrication of the hardware keep them working smoothly.

Are uPVC windows suitable for cold areas of Himachal?

Yes. uPVC is one of the best materials for cold regions. Its multi-chamber profiles and airtight gaskets, especially with double glazing, reduce heat loss and keep rooms warmer.
