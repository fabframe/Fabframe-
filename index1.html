Why aluminium systems

Performance Benefits for Modern Buildings

Aluminium frames are widely selected for modern residential and commercial projects because they combine structural strength, slim visual lines and low-maintenance performance.

Learn more about aluminium system selection through the FabFrame aluminium window design guide .

Slim Profiles

Narrow frame lines allow wider glass areas and improved natural-light access.

Structural Strength

Aluminium provides a strong frame solution for varied window and door opening sizes.

Low Maintenance

Aluminium systems do not require the recurring polishing associated with traditional wooden frames.

Weather Resistance

Suitable profiles and drainage planning help systems perform across changing climatic conditions.

Design Flexibility

Sliding, casement, fixed, French and multi-track formats can support different architectural concepts.

Glass Compatibility

Aluminium systems can be planned with suitable glazing based on privacy, comfort and project requirements.
