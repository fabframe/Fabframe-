Six Details That Improve Wardrobe Planning

1. Accurate Measurements

Record wall width, ceiling height, depth, skirting, sockets, windows and door movement.

2. Storage Categories

Separate long hanging, short hanging, folded clothes, drawers, luggage, accessories and shoes.

3. Shutter Selection

Choose sliding shutters for limited clearance or openable shutters for full internal access.

4. Finish Coordination

Match the wardrobe palette with flooring, wall colours, furniture and overall interior character.

5. Site Location

Share whether the site is in Una, Chandigarh, Punjab, Haryana or another service location.

6. Daily Convenience

Keep frequently used storage at practical heights and reserve upper areas for seasonal items.
