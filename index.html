Wardrobe planning checklist

Details to Finalise Before Ordering a Wardrobe

Accurate Measurements

Record wall width, height, depth, skirting, switches, windows and nearby door movement.

Storage Categories

Plan separate areas for hanging clothes, folded items, drawers, luggage, accessories and shoes.

Shutter Selection

Select sliding shutters for compact rooms or openable shutters for complete internal access.

Interior Coordination

Match the wardrobe finish with flooring, wall colours, furniture and the overall interior theme.

Project Location

Share whether the project is in Una, Shimla, Chandigarh, Mohali, Punjab, Haryana or another North India location.

Daily Convenience

Keep frequently used storage at practical heights and reserve upper shelves for seasonal items.
